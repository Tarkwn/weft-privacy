Weft: Fill-In Puzzles — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Weft: Fill-In Puzzles handles user data. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

Weft does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your puzzle progress and settings are stored only on your device. The following data may be collected automatically through the third-party services listed below:

  • Device and app identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set ID (used by the advertising providers), and a Firebase installation ID (used to deliver app configuration).
  • Approximate location: estimated from your IP address by the advertising providers.
  • App interactions: events such as app launches, ad taps, and ad video views, collected by the advertising providers.
  • Diagnostics: performance information such as app launch time and hang rate, collected by the advertising providers.
  • Game services data: if you use the leaderboard, your Google Play Games player profile and your score are processed by Google Play Games.

All of this data is encrypted in transit.

Third-Party Services

  • Unity LevelPlay (with ironSource Ads and Unity Ads) — delivers interstitial ads between puzzles and optional rewarded ads you can watch for an extra hint. Collects the data listed above and shares it with its advertising partners for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform — may serve ads through the mediation above, and shows the consent message where required by law. Shares the data listed above with Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Google Play Games Services — leaderboard and player profile.
  • Firebase Remote Config — delivers game configuration, such as update notices.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional “Remove Ads” purchase. Payment details are handled by Google Play; we never receive them.
  • Google Play In-App Review — may occasionally ask you to rate the game. Ratings are submitted directly to Google Play.

Google services are provided by Google LLC under the Google Privacy Policy. Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads are provided by Unity Technologies under the Unity Privacy Policy.

How We Use Data

Collected data is used only to operate the game, fix bugs, measure performance, improve the experience, and show ads. We do not sell your data.

Children's Privacy

Weft is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Data Security and Retention

Data is processed under the security standards of the respective third-party providers and retained only as long as necessary for the stated purposes.

Your Rights

You can reset or turn off ad personalization from your device settings. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before ads are requested, and you can review or change that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy in the game. Contact us for any requests regarding your data.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes take effect once published on this page.
