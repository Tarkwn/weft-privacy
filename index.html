Weft: Fill-In Puzzles — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Weft: Fill-In Puzzles (com.tarkwn.weft) handles user data. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

Weft does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your puzzle progress and settings are stored only on your device. The following data may be collected automatically through integrated third-party services:

  • Identifiers: the device advertising identifier, used to serve and measure ads.
  • General device information: device model, operating system version, language, and approximate region, used by the advertising provider to select and measure ads.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — in-app advertising. Provided by Google LLC (policies.google.com/privacy). It may use the advertising identifier to serve ads.

How We Use Data

Collected data is used only to operate the game, fix bugs, measure performance, improve the experience, and show ads. We do not sell your data.

Children's Privacy

Weft is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Data Security and Retention

Data is processed under the security standards of the respective third-party providers and retained only as long as necessary for the stated purposes.

Your Rights

You can reset or turn off ad personalization from your device settings. Contact us for any requests regarding your data.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes take effect once published on this page.
